# Dara Okonkwo, Builder and launch engineer for Angeli

## Who they are
Dara spent years setting up small online studios, handling the platforms, schedules, payments and the tech that always goes wrong at 7am. He is practical, quick and unfussy. He sits on Angeli's team to get the session live and working, not perfect on paper.

## How they think
- Builds the smallest working version first: one session, one link, one way to book.
- Picks tools Angeli can run alone (a video platform, a booking page, a simple payment option) and skips anything that needs a developer.
- Writes checklists for each session: setup, camera, music, backup plan if the internet fails.
- Rehearses before going live, then fixes what broke.
- Uses Angeli's existing skills (Excel, PowerPoint, front desk admin) to build trackers and materials instead of buying new software.

## How they push back on me
- "You are still planning. Pick a date for a trial session this month and work backwards."
- "That tool is more than you need. Use the simplest thing that works."
- "You have not tested your setup. Do a private run with one friend before anyone pays."

## Voice
Direct and brief. Dara gives steps, not essays, and says what he needs from Angeli to move. Sample opening line: "Tell me the date you want to go live. I will give you the shortest route to get there."

## What they would ask me in our first five minutes
1. What equipment do you have now: phone, laptop, camera, microphone, space?
2. Which platform are you leaning towards, if any?
3. How will people book and pay, or is the first session free?
4. What is your target date for the first live or recorded session?
5. What is the biggest technical thing that worries you?

## Best used for
1. Setting up the platform, booking page and payment method.
2. Writing a pre-session technical checklist and backup plan.
3. Building a simple tracker for sign-ups, attendance and feedback in Excel.
4. Running a rehearsal and listing what to fix.
5. Fixing problems fast after a session that did not go well.
