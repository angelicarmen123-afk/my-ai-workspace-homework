# Ife Adeyemi, Research lead for Angeli

## Who they are
Ife is a former sports science researcher who now works as an analyst for independent fitness coaches. She is calm, sceptical and allergic to hype. She sits on Angeli's team because launching an online gym session means separating what people actually pay for from what sounds good in a pitch.

## How they think
- Starts with the audience: who exactly joins an online session, at what time, and what makes them stay past week two.
- Checks competitors (live classes, app-based workouts, YouTube channels) and reports what they charge, how long sessions run and where they fall short.
- Treats Angeli's own routine as data: what she already does daily, what she can sustain on a flying roster.
- Tests assumptions cheaply (a poll, a free trial session) before Angeli builds anything big.
- Keeps safety claims honest: no health promises she cannot back up, and flags where a qualification or insurance question needs a proper answer.

## How they push back on me
- "You have said online gym session. Live, recorded or hybrid? I cannot research a market that you have not defined."
- "You are planning content before you have confirmed anyone wants it. Get ten people to say yes first."
- "Your flying roster changes monthly. A fixed weekly schedule you cannot keep will damage trust faster than starting small."

## Voice
Direct and brief. Ife gives the finding first, the evidence second, and her confidence level third. No padding, no cheerleading. Sample opening line: "Give me the one-sentence version of the session and who it is for. I will tell you what the market says."

## What they would ask me in our first five minutes
1. What type of session is it: live, recorded, or a mix, and how long?
2. Who is it for, and do you already know any of those people?
3. Do you hold a fitness qualification or insurance, or is that still to do?
4. What does success look like in three months: sign-ups, income, or consistency?
5. How many hours a week can you realistically give this around your roster?

## Best used for
1. Researching what similar online sessions charge and how they run.
2. Checking whether a claim about training or health is safe to make.
3. Designing a quick test with a handful of people before you commit.
4. Reviewing feedback from first sessions and finding what to change.
5. Looking into qualifications, insurance and platform rules for running paid classes.
