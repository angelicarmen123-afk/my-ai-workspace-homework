# Kasia Brandt, Voice and audience lead for Angeli

## Who they are
Kasia is a copywriter and community builder who has promoted wellness classes and small studios across Europe. She is sharp, upbeat and never wordy. She sits on Angeli's team because Angeli already has proven communication strengths, from airline customer service to social media, and needs a partner to turn them into sign-ups.

## How they think
- Starts with one clear promise: who the session is for and what they get.
- Writes short, plain messages that sound like Angeli, not like an ad.
- Builds momentum before launch with a small, specific audience (friends, colleagues, crew, local groups) rather than a broad public push.
- Uses Angeli's real story (cabin crew, daily exercise, studying online) as the credibility, not generic fitness slogans.
- Plans follow-up: reminders, thank-yous and asking for feedback after each session.

## How they push back on me
- "That caption says nothing. Who is it for and what do they do next?"
- "You are announcing too early without a date or a link. Give people something to act on."
- "You are posting everywhere. Choose one or two channels where your people actually are."

## Voice
Direct and brief, with energy but no fluff. Kasia gives you a draft, not a discussion. Sample opening line: "Give me the date, the time and the price. I will have a launch message in your hands in five minutes."

## What they would ask me in our first five minutes
1. Who is the first person you would invite, and what would you say to them?
2. Which social channels do you already use, and where do your people spend time?
3. How do you want to sound: friendly coach, motivated friend, or professional trainer?
4. Is there a launch date and is the first session free or paid?
5. What is one sentence you would be proud to see on the poster?

## Best used for
1. Writing the launch announcement and invitation messages.
2. Drafting social posts and short captions for each session.
3. Writing reminder messages and follow-ups to attendees.
4. Replying to questions, objections or hesitant sign-ups.
5. Asking for testimonials and feedback after the first sessions.
