# Post-Session Follow-Up & Testimonial Generator

## When to use
- Right after a live or recorded gym session ends to message attendees.
- When you need to turn quick session notes into warm, personalized follow-ups.
- When asking past attendees for quick testimonials or feedback.
- Do not use for initial launch announcements or marketing campaign strategy.

## Inputs needed
- List of attendees and quick notes (Required. If missing, ask: "Who attended the session, and what is one brief note or highlight for each person?")
- Next session date and booking link (Required. If missing, ask: "What is the date and link for the next session?")

## Steps
1. Review the attendee notes and separate first-time attendees from returning regulars.
2. Draft a personalized 2 to 3-sentence thank-you message for each participant.
3. Include the specific highlight or positive observation mentioned in the input notes.
4. Add a low-pressure request for a short 1-sentence feedback or testimonial[cite: 7].
5. Append the booking link for the upcoming session.
6. Format the output into copy-pasteable blocks with participant names clearly labeled.

## Output contract
- Length: 3 to 4 sentences per attendee.
- Format: Labeled text blocks ready to copy into WhatsApp, SMS, or direct messages.
- What must be in the output: Warm greeting, specific note/highlight, feedback/testimonial question, next link.
- What must not be in the output: Generic marketing slogans, lengthy sales pitches, or artificial cheerleading[cite: 7].
- Example skeleton:
  **For [Name]:**
  Hi [Name]! Thanks so much for joining today's session. You did an amazing job with [highlight note]! If you enjoyed it, I'd love a quick 1-sentence testimonial on how you felt after. Here is the link to reserve your spot for next week: [Link].

## One worked example
- Inputs: 
  * Sarah (first-timer, great form on lunges)
  * Mark (regular, pushed through the final core set)
  * Next session: Thursday 7 PM (link: gym.link/thu)
- Output:
  **For Sarah:**
  Hi Sarah! Thanks so much for joining your first session today! Your form on those lunges was spot on. If you enjoyed the workout, I’d love a quick 1-sentence review of how you felt afterwards. Here’s the link if you’d like to join us again next Thursday at 7 PM: gym.link/thu

  **For Mark:**
  Hey Mark! Great seeing you back today! Way to power through that final core set at the end. If you have 30 seconds, I'd love a quick line on how the progressive workouts are feeling for you so far. Here's the link to lock in your spot for Thursday: gym.link/thu
